# Separate administrator identity from management location

Across all plugins, centrally managed administrators and a fixed minimum Koishi authority of 4 determine who may access administrator-only data, while configured management groups determine where state-changing chat management actions may run. Administrator-only reads are available to an administrator in any group or private chat. Group membership alone grants no administrative power, preventing a configured operations group from implicitly authorizing every member; private state changes still require an authorized user. An empty management-group set denies state changes in every group rather than making every group a management location. The authority threshold is deliberately not configurable.
